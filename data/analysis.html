ITAR and EAR are not the same barrier. ITAR (defense articles) requires US-person status outright. EAR (dual-use technology) works by country group, and Canada is in the most-favoured group — so an EAR clause is usually satisfiable where an ITAR one is not. This cuts both ways for a dual national: EAR asks which country a release counts as going to, so a second citizenship in an embargoed country (Country Group E:1 — Iran, Cuba, North Korea, Syria) or in D:1 (China, Russia and others) can turn an otherwise-fine EAR role into a blocked one. 1 posting(s) could not be read and are excluded from the percentages rather than assumed clear.

Method notes

  • Only open postings (closed=0) are counted. Pinned standing postings (e.g. 'Internships / Co-op' pages) count as one.
